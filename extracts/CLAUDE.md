# Extracted Artifacts — Staging Area

Staged artifacts produced by `/extract-artifacts` (non-pattern forms) and `/synthesize-guide` (pattern-aggregated guides). Everything here is **draft** — not yet deployed to enforcement locations.

**Lifecycle:** Artifacts stage here after classification (`/identify-artifacts`) and drafting. Nick reviews, then deploys approved artifacts to their target locations (e.g., `knowledge/guides/`, `.claude/rules/`).

**Subdirectories by form:** `patterns/`, `guides/`, `rules/`, `skills/`, `templates/`, `agents/`. Each form has its own deployment target per DD-81.

**Do not deploy from here automatically.** Human gate required (DD-29).
