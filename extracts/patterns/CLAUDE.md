**These are NOT the final consumable.** Per DD-81, pattern findings route to `/synthesize-guide` for aggregation into end-directed guides. These pattern files serve as enrichment input during guide synthesis — the structured Forces/Tradeoffs sections help the guide author synthesize across findings.

**Do not deploy these to `knowledge/patterns/`.** Guides are the deployable product for pattern-classified findings.
