# CareerBuddy improve-backlog — 5 corpus contributions queued for the engine

CareerBuddy (Nick's career-artifact agentic system) maintains an improvement backlog at
`ops/self/improve-backlog.md`; its "corpus upstreaming" entry (operator-owned, from the
swept MV21 Phase R brief) names five candidate contributions to `nickgogan/improvement-loop`.
Intake chased each item past the backlog one-liner into the repo itself — `onboarding/system-contract.yaml`
and its prose companion, `ADAPTATION.md` (receiving-agent protocol), canon doc
`onboarding/08-operational-quirks.md`, the `meta-harness-author` wiring vocabulary, the
staged platform adapters (vscode-copilot, cursor, codex, claude-code INSTALL-MAPs), and
the MV21/MV20 milestone records in HISTORY.md — so each finding captures shipped
mechanics and motivating evidence, not just the queue line. Four findings were created;
the fifth contribution (card-over-manifest fusion) extended the existing
`machine-readable-system-contract-with-wiring-rows.md` finding, which already documented
the contract it frames. The Pi trio finding is evidence-Low by design: the primary Phase R
research brief was swept at ship and the clone is shallow, so it documents the surviving
fingerprints and flags re-research from Pi primary sources.
