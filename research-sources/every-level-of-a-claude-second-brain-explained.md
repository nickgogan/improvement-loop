# Every Level of a Claude Second Brain Explained

Nate Herk (Nate Herk | AI Automation) walks his production second brain ("Herc 2" — plain
markdown files and folders, tool-agnostic across Claude Code, Codex, and Hermes Agent)
through a five-level retrieval maturity ladder, arguing that level selection is per-folder
and pain-driven, not a system-wide upgrade path. He runs his own system at level 2 (routing
files + LLM wiki) and explicitly declines levels 4-5 for lack of felt pain.
