  domains — Deloitte, McKinsey repositioning as AI-assurance; 11 Labs offering voice-agent insurance; Viva and Elation as regulated SaaS). Meta-pattern: AI commoditizes production; durability lives in layers
  production CAN''T replace. Core strategic test: ''What do I own that still matters if AI gets 10 times better?'' If answer is nothing, reposition now. Direct relevance to MetaSystem: framework for strategic
  positioning of any MetaSystem-built business or agent capability.'
