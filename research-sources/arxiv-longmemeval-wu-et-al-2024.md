Authors propose a three-stage framework (indexing, retrieval, reading) with session decomposition, fact-augmented key expansion, and time-aware query expansion. CC BY 4.0 licensed. This is the primary-source paper behind every LongMemEval claim across MemPalace, Supermemory, Hindsight, REM Labs, Mem0, Zep, and Memongo."
