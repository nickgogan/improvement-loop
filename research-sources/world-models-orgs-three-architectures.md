# World Models for Orgs — 3 Architectures, Interpretive Boundary Layer

Nate B Jones responds to Jack Dorsey's viral world-model blueprint by dissecting the three real architectures behind the concept: (1) vector DB / semantic retrieval — fast to deploy, adequate for information logistics, but ranking is silent interpretation; (2) structured ontology (Palantir model) — precise within schema, blind to emergent patterns; (3) signal fidelity (Block/Dorsey model) — clean inputs create an illusion of authoritative causal inference. All three share a structural failure: no explicit boundary between information surfacing and judgment. The video proposes the interpretive boundary layer as the missing architectural element and closes with five compounding principles: signal fidelity ceiling, structure earned not imposed, outcome encoding, designing for resistance, and time as the primary moat.
