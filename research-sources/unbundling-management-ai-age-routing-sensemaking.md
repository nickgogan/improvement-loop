# Unbundling Management in the AI Age: Routing, Sensemaking, and Accountability

Nate B Jones breaks down the three core functions bundled inside "management" — routing (information logistics), sensemaking (signal-from-noise translation), and accountability/feedback (ownership and coaching) — and assesses AI automatability for each. He then examines three live organizational experiments: Kimi/Moonshot AI (agents + 5 co-founders + no accountability), Block/Jack Dorsey (AI world model + DRI rotation + player-coaches), and Meta (AI-assisted routing + compressed human hierarchy + intensified accountability). The decompose-before-compress principle is the core takeaway: removing management without explicitly reassigning all three functions produces drift, anxiety, and attrition regardless of AI capability gains on routing.
