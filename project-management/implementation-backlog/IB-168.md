# IB-168: MetaSystem Owner operational skill family

The MetaSystem Owner exists (IB-167, session 112) but operates without a dedicated skill family. The IL Owner skill family is hardcoded to IL paths and agent rosters; the question is per-skill whether to parameterize (single source) or build equivalents (clean separation).

## Why P2

Not blocking — MetaSystem Owner operates via direct Read/Glob/Grep/Write/Edit/Bash for now. Becomes load-bearing as MetaSystem-level operations (audits, doc maintenance, drift detection) accumulate frequency. Rule 11 — wait for recurring evidence before committing to either pattern.
