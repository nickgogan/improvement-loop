# IB-171: Corpus-wide KB linkage-hygiene sweep

Deferred by Nick at session 137 ("save for later"). The batch-scoped hygiene passes
fixed the session-136 debt but surfaced a much larger pre-existing condition across
the whole findings/sources corpus.

## Scope (as measured 2026-07-12, post-batch-fix)

- ~992 one-way `related_findings` links missing their reciprocal (scriptable backfill).
- 205 typed mismatches — both files link each other but with disagreeing rel types;
  needs per-pair adjudication (subagent fan-out).
- 53 links using non-canonical rel vocabulary outside the 4-type contract
  (companion, depends-on, contrasts-with, feeds-into, related, part-contains,
  solved-by, mitigates, same-technique) — normalize or extend the contract (Rule 11
  question).
- 6 dangling link targets (renamed/deleted files) + 24 broken source↔finding refs.
- 150 findings with empty `sources` — triage legitimate (repo-born) vs broken.
- 11 unlinked sources; 16 over-cap hub findings (>15 links, worst 42) needing the
  soft-cap judgment call.
- Optional rider: new-link pair evaluation over the session-136 finding batch
  (skipped in 137 on token-economy grounds).

## How to approach

Run as one dedicated sweep session with extended autonomy. Reciprocal backfill and
dangling-ref repair are scriptable mechanics; mismatch adjudication and vocabulary
normalization need subagent evaluation with the /finding-crosslink binary tests;
hub-cap and vocabulary-contract questions gate on Nick. Detection scripts and full
inventories: `operations/research-reports/2026-07-12-crosslink-reciprocity-session-137.md`
and `2026-07-12-linkage-repair-session-137.md`.
