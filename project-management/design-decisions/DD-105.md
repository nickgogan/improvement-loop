# DD-105

> **For agents:** Read `CHARTER.md` (workspace root) before architectural work — it carries vision, mission, purpose, values, and trajectory signals. It is NOT operating law (that's `CLAUDE.md` / `.claude/rules/` / engine `governance/`) and NOT design-wisdom (that's `knowledge/reference/`). When deciding whether a proposed change keeps the engine on-path, check it against the charter's **trajectory signals** — the paired on-track / wandering lists are the test. Nick authors charter content; the Owner runs the edit mechanics (proposal-first).

---

## Core Decision

The collapse (DD-103) retired the meta-system governance documents (constitution, values, principles). Their durable content was distilled into a single root-level **charter**. The charter is defined by what it is *not* as much as what it is:

| Layer | Home | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Charter** | `CHARTER.md` (root) | Vision / mission / purpose / values / **trajectory signals** — where we're going and how we know we're on track |
| Operating law | `CLAUDE.md`, `.claude/rules/`, engine `governance/` | Enforced rules (human gate, spec-before-build, boundaries, safety) |
| Design-wisdom | `knowledge/reference/{fractal-pattern,principles,vocabulary}.md` | Consumed knowledge (fractal pattern, DBDO pipeline, vocabulary) — not enforced |

The charter does not restate operating law or design-wisdom. It is short by design.

---

## The distinguishing element: trajectory signals

The charter's load-bearing innovation over the retired constitution is an explicit **trajectory-signals** section — paired lists of *on-track* and *wandering* indicators. Examples (see `CHARTER.md` for the full set):

- *On track:* new abstractions arrive with evidence (recurring problem + named consumer); artifacts are research-grounded; the engine is bilingual (what it audits, it can design); self-evolution is observable; maintenance stays bounded.
- *Wandering:* abstractions built for elegance or a single speculative consumer; findings that never reach an artifact; audit/design surfaces drifting out of parity; scope committed ahead of demand.

This converts "stay true to the vision" from a feeling into a checkable signal set — answering the charter's own framing question, "how do we know we're on track vs. wandering?"

---

## Placement: the fractal exception

The charter is the **only deliberate exception** to the DD-52 7-folder fractal. Unit-scoped governance lives in each unit's `governance/`; the charter scopes the *entire workspace*, so it sits at the workspace root rather than inside any unit. This exception is named here and in DD-103's DD-52 amendment so it is not read as drift.

---

## Stewardship

Per DD-44 and DD-86: Nick is the **content** author of the charter; the Owner agent runs the **mechanics** (drafting, edits, cross-reference propagation) proposal-first — when collaborating with Nick inline, the conversation is the gate and the Owner writes directly; when initiating without Nick present, the Owner drafts in `governance/proposals/` for Nick's later gate. The charter-stewardship responsibility was folded into the single engine Owner in the collapse (Step 6).

## Why

A self-evolving engine needs a fixed point to evolve *toward*, or "self-improvement" degenerates into drift dressed as progress. The retired constitution stated rules but never stated how to tell on-path from off-path. The trajectory signals close that gap, and a root-level placement keeps the vision visible above the unit it governs.
