# DD-120

> **For agents:** Looking for the agent-team role model or the Explore/Harden quality bars? They live in `knowledge/patterns/composable-agent-teams.md` and `knowledge/patterns/explore-then-harden.md`. DD-60/DD-62 are Superseded carriers, not current law — the engine's own actor model is DD-82; its gate model is DD-29/DD-119.

---

## The Constraint

- DD-60 → `knowledge/patterns/composable-agent-teams.md` (roles table, composability tiers, persona specification, project-vs-workspace scoping; template path updated from the dissolved `systems/meta-system/...` to `knowledge/templates/agent-templates/`).
- DD-62 → `knowledge/patterns/explore-then-harden.md` (phase tables, role responsibilities, phase-boundary criteria).
- Both DDs: `status: Superseded`, `superseded_by: DD-120`. Content is moved, not amended — any future change to the methodology happens in the pattern docs (knowledge lifecycle), not via DD amendment.

## Why

The substrate audit (gate G3) classified both as governance-misfiled methodology: a DD binds what the *system* does; these describe what a *project the engine helps design* should do. Under the kernel litmus they are teachable patterns (downstream consumers pull them as guidance, not as law). Keeping them Binding overstated the rulebook and froze living methodology behind the DD-immutability rule.
