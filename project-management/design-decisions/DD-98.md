# DD-98

> **For agents:** When a guide crosses BOTH thresholds (≥25 findings AND ≥2 distinct practitioner questions), Codifier emits a split proposal at `operations/split-proposals/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<guide-stem>-split-proposal.md`. The proposal is read-only — never split a guide without a Nick-approved DD specifying the destinations and the per-finding bifurcation. The source guide's changelog gets a final `guide-split` entry; each destination opens its own changelog with a `guide-split` initial entry pointing back to the source.

---

## The Constraint

**Trigger — conjunction, both required.**

A guide qualifies for split-proposal review when:

1. **Finding-count threshold.** The guide's current `source_findings[]` (or its routed cluster as enumerated by the routing table) contains **≥25 findings**.
2. **Practitioner-question threshold.** The findings cluster around **≥2 distinct practitioner questions** — bifurcation is structural, not just volumetric.

A guide that crosses (1) without (2) does NOT qualify. G4 (Evaluation) at 32 findings is the canonical counterexample — a single coherent practitioner question (`How do I verify my agent actually works?`) carries the full mass without strain.

A guide that crosses (2) without (1) does NOT qualify. Volume is the cost gate; without it, the friction of splitting exceeds the friction of carrying two questions inside one guide.

**Detection.** Codifier checks the trigger on every `/identify-artifacts` run and on every `/synthesize-guide` Step 0 read of the routing table. Detection is on-demand at these natural read points; no separate periodic sweep.

**Response — propose, don't execute.**

On trigger, Codifier emits a structured proposal at `operations/split-proposals/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<guide-stem>-split-proposal.md`. The proposal contains:

- **Source guide identity** — current title, finding count, routing-table row.
- **Practitioner-question analysis** — the ≥2 distinct questions identified, with the findings clustering against each.
- **Proposed bifurcation** — destination guide names, per-finding routing (one finding to one destination, or flagged as shared/contested), proposed practitioner questions.
- **Preserved-section disposition** — for each `## Nick's Annotations` block and each `<!-- PRESERVE -->` region in the source guide (DD-93), a recommendation: route to destination A, route to destination B, or duplicate to both with rationale.
- **Routing-table impact** — proposed deprecation row for source; proposed new rows for destinations; dimension-mapping changes if any.
- **Codifier recommendation** — `proceed with split as proposed` | `defer pending more findings` | `re-evaluate practitioner-question bifurcation` | `absorb into adjacent guide instead`.

The proposal is **never** auto-executed. Splitting a guide requires a Nick-approved DD that codifies the destination structure.

**Per-split DD.** Each executed split is governed by its own DD (one DD per split). The split DD specifies:

- The two (or more) destination guide names and their practitioner questions.
- The exact per-finding bifurcation (which finding goes to which destination).
- The disposition of preserved sections.
- The source guide's deprecation marker.

The split DD inherits Phase-3 lifecycle authority from DD-98; DD-98 is the procedural parent, not the per-split substantive ruling.

## Why

**Volume is necessary but not sufficient.** A 25-finding guide with a single practitioner question is coherent — the question is dense, the answers are layered. Splitting it forks one mental model into two artificial halves. The practitioner-question threshold filters out volumetric splits that don't earn their cost.

**Practitioner-question count is necessary but not sufficient.** A 12-finding guide that touches two practitioner questions is still small enough that the dual focus is manageable in one document — the cost of forking it into two thin guides exceeds the cost of carrying both questions in one. The volume threshold filters out premature splits that would create undersized destinations.

**The conjunction matches observed corpus dynamics.** As of session 73 close: G2 at 26 findings with one focused question (`My agent is losing context or burning tokens`) — qualifies on (1), not on (2). G4 at 32 findings with one question — qualifies on (1), not on (2). No active guide currently qualifies on both. The threshold exists to catch the future case where mass and bifurcation coincide; the current corpus stress-tests the conservatism.

**Why a separate DD per split, not blanket authority.** A split is a structural change to the guide cluster — it forks consumer-facing identity, deprecates a routing path, and creates two new clusters that each need their own coverage profile. The blast radius is high enough that Nick's per-split read of "is this the right bifurcation?" is structurally required (DD-29 stage-boundary gate). DD-98 governs the procedure; the per-split DD governs the substance.

**Why Codifier proposes, not Nick proposes.** The trigger detection is corpus-mechanical (count + question-cluster analysis); routing it through Nick's attention before a proposal is drafted creates an avoidable Nick-as-trigger bottleneck (standing feedback principle: reduce Nick input). Codifier's proposal is the read-once artifact; Nick's gate is the rule-once gate.

## Rules for Codifier

1. **Detect on natural read points.** Check the split trigger when `/identify-artifacts` reads the routing table for unrouted-bucket review, and when `/synthesize-guide` Step 0 reads the routing table for cluster resolution. No separate sweep skill needed.

2. **Both thresholds, no shortcuts.** Trigger requires count ≥25 AND practitioner-question count ≥2. Single-condition detection is NOT a trigger. Codifier may surface a single-condition observation as informational ("G2 is at 26 findings with one practitioner question — monitor for question bifurcation") but does NOT emit a split proposal.

3. **Propose via report file, not frontmatter.** Split proposals live as standalone files under `operations/split-proposals/`. The source guide's frontmatter is NOT modified by the proposal — the proposal is fully external to the guide artifact.

4. **Bifurcation precision.** Every finding in the source guide must be routed in the proposal: to destination A, to destination B, or flagged as `shared` (proposed to both) or `contested` (Codifier cannot route confidently). Findings without disposition are a proposal defect.

5. **Preserved-section disposition is explicit.** Every `## Nick's Annotations` block and every `<!-- PRESERVE -->` region in the source guide gets an explicit recommendation: route to A, route to B, or duplicate to both. Implicit handling is forbidden — DD-93's preservation invariant requires explicit choice on split.

6. **Never auto-execute.** Codifier never writes the destination guide files, never updates the routing table, never deprecates the source guide, and never modifies DD-94's changelog files based on its own proposal. Execution requires a Nick-approved DD.

## Split Execution (Post-Approval)

After the per-split DD lands, the executing skill (`/synthesize-guide` for the destinations; manual for routing-table and changelog updates) performs:

1. **Source guide.**
   - Update frontmatter: `stage: deprecated`, retain all other fields.
   - Append final changelog entry: trigger tag `guide-split`, body lines naming destinations and SL link.
   - Source body retained intact (no content deletion); preservation regions remain in place per the DD's disposition.

2. **Destination guides.**
   - Each destination created at `extracts/guides/<destination-stem>.md` via `/synthesize-guide` running on the destination's bifurcated finding set per the split DD.
   - Each destination opens a new companion changelog at `extracts/guides/changelog/<destination-stem>.changelog.md`.
   - Each destination's first changelog entry uses trigger tag `guide-split` with a body line naming the source guide and citing the split DD.
   - Preserved sections from the source are inserted per the DD's per-region disposition (route-to-A, route-to-B, or duplicate-to-both).

3. **Routing table.**
   - Source guide's row in Active Clusters → moved to a new "Deprecated Clusters" subsection (or marked deprecated in place; routing table editor's choice — codified separately if needed).
   - Two new rows added for destinations with their practitioner questions and finding counts.
   - Dimension → Guide mapping table updated if the split changes dimension routing.

## Trigger-Tag Enum Amendment to DD-94

DD-94's `## The Constraint` section defines a closed enum of trigger tags for guide changelog headers. DD-98 amends that enum to add one tag:

- **`guide-split`** — used in two contexts:
  - **Source guide's final entry.** Body line: "Split into destinations [[<destination-A-stem>]] and [[<destination-B-stem>]] per [[DD-XXX]]" where DD-XXX is the per-split DD.
  - **Destination guide's first entry.** Body line: "Derived from source [[<source-stem>]] per [[DD-XXX]]" where DD-XXX is the per-split DD.

DD-94's amendment process is documented in this DD's body — no separate edit to DD-94 is required, but the DD-94 file's `## The Constraint` enum bullet list is updated in the Phase-3 IB sweep that implements DD-98 (Phase-3 IB work, future session — not session 74).

The amendment honors DD-94's "new trigger tags require a DD amendment, not ad-hoc invention" rule — DD-98 IS the DD amendment.

## Failure Modes and Recovery

| Failure mode | Detection | Recovery |
|---|---|---|
| Trigger fires (≥25 findings) but practitioner-question bifurcation is unclear | Codifier's analysis cannot identify ≥2 distinct questions; the cluster is genuinely single-question | NO proposal emitted. Codifier emits a routing-table observation only ("G-N at <count> findings; remains single-question"). Continue monitoring on subsequent reads. |
| Trigger fires; practitioner questions are clear but findings do not separate cleanly (significant `shared` set) | The proposal's bifurcation table shows >20% of findings as `shared` or `contested` | Codifier surfaces this as the proposal's primary finding; recommendation field is `re-evaluate practitioner-question bifurcation`. Nick rules: defer (re-cluster on next intake), absorb shared findings into one destination by judgment, or split with explicit duplication. |
| Source guide has preserved sections whose content spans both destinations | Codifier identifies preserved content semantically relevant to both questions | Disposition recommendation = `duplicate to both` with explicit rationale. Nick rules per-region. |
| Source guide is mid-`/synthesize-guide` regen when split trigger fires | Detection happens at Step 0; regen is in flight | The regen completes against the source guide's existing structure. Split proposal is emitted at the end of the regen, citing the post-regen finding count. The regen's changelog entry uses its normal trigger (`staleness-threshold`, etc.); the split proposal is a separate artifact. |
| Per-split DD lands but execution skill diverges from DD's bifurcation | Post-execution audit finds finding-routing mismatch | Procedural violation; flagged in next governance audit. Recovery: revert via git; re-execute against the DD's specification. |
| Trigger fires repeatedly on a guide Nick has explicitly chosen NOT to split (e.g., "G4 stays whole") | Codifier emits proposals on every detection cycle | Codifier reads the source guide's frontmatter for an opt-out marker. If Nick adds `split_opt_out: true` to a guide's frontmatter, Codifier suppresses split proposals for that guide and emits a one-line observation in the routing table instead. (The opt-out mechanism is not yet codified in `_schema.yaml`; Phase-3 IB sweep adds it if/when needed.) |

## Acceptance Criteria

| Test | Expected |
|---|---|
| Guide reaches ≥25 findings with a single coherent practitioner question | No proposal emitted. Codifier may surface a routing-table observation. |
| Guide reaches ≥25 findings AND covers ≥2 distinct practitioner questions | Proposal file written at `operations/split-proposals/<YYYY-MM-DD>-<guide-stem>-split-proposal.md` with per-finding bifurcation and preserved-section disposition. |
| Codifier emits a split proposal that auto-executes the split (writes destination files) | Procedural violation; flagged in next governance audit. The proposal is read-only by contract. |
| Per-split DD approved; execution skill creates destinations | Source marked `stage: deprecated`; final `guide-split` entry on source's changelog; destination guides created with first `guide-split` entries; routing table updated. |
| Source guide has preserved sections; per-split DD specifies disposition per region | All regions land at the destinations specified; no region is dropped. |
| Codifier emits a proposal without explicit disposition for one preserved region | Proposal is defective; Nick may reject for re-emission or rule on disposition inline. |
| Source guide is `stage: deprecated` post-split; new findings later route to its dimension | Routing table directs to the destinations, not the deprecated source. Deprecated guides do not accept new findings. |

## Scope and Non-Goals

- **In scope:** All guides in `extracts/guides/` subject to `/synthesize-guide`. The split procedure governs the lifecycle event of bifurcating one guide into two (or more — the procedure generalizes to N destinations, though 2 is the expected case).
- **In scope:** The DD-94 trigger-tag enum amendment adding `guide-split`.
- **Out of scope:** Guide MERGE — the inverse operation (two thin guides combining into one). Spec §2.3 mentions guide merge as a possibility but does not codify a trigger; not addressed by DD-98. A future DD may codify merge if the corpus develops the case.
- **Out of scope:** Auto-detection of practitioner-question bifurcation. The "≥2 distinct practitioner questions" check is LLM-judgmental, performed by Codifier at proposal time. No structured rubric for what counts as "distinct" — calibration in line with DD-97's loose-trigger philosophy. False-positives caught at Nick's gate.
- **Out of scope:** Numeric tuning. The 25 / 2 thresholds are spec-defined; this DD codifies them as-is. If observed corpus dynamics show the thresholds are wrong, amend in a follow-up DD with evidence.
- **Out of scope:** Live-location lifecycle. The deprecated source guide's deployment to `meta-system/knowledge/guides/` (if deployed) is governed by DD-29; DD-98 only addresses the staging artifact.
- **Out of scope:** Cross-form splits. A guide is the only form that splits — rules / skills / templates / agents do not split (they parameterize per DD-97 or version per DD-100).
