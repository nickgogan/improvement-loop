# DD-92

> **For agents:** Every extracted or deployed artifact gets a ContextSpec block in frontmatter. Universal vocabulary only — no MetaSystem-specific labels. Companion to DD-78's ContractSpec.

---

## The Constraint

ContextSpec is a frontmatter-only block; all fields required unless explicitly optional:

```yaml
context:
  applies_to:            # [plain English strings] — consumer-context list; narrative, not enum
  platform_coupling:     # "agnostic" | "specific:<platform>"
  autonomy:              # "all" | "hitl-only" | "autonomous-only"
  stage:                 # "specify" | "build" | "verify" | "secure" | "operate"
  reversibility:         # "trivial — <note>" | "low — <note>" | "medium — <note>" | "high — <note>" | "irreversible — <note>"
  auditability:          # plain English: how externally verifiable is compliance
  evidence_strength:     # "Low" | "Medium" | "Strong"
  adoption:
    status:              # "Not Yet Started" | "Partially Adopted" | "Adopted"
    notes:               # plain English: past-signal — where this has been applied, with what outcomes. Optional.
```

No `## Applicability` section in the body. No dedicated `## Anti-patterns` section — counter-indications live in whatever existing section fits (ContractSpec.preconditions, body Condition, body Failure Modes).

## Universal Vocabulary Constraint

ContextSpec fields must be readable by consumers outside MetaSystem. Forbidden:

- MetaSystem scope labels (`S2`, `S3`, `General` as shorthand for IL applicability bookkeeping)
- IL-internal skill names (`/assess-skill`, `/identify-artifacts`) — use generic descriptors ("skill-assessment rubrics", "classification tooling")
- System-specific file references (`CLAUDE.md` is borderline — Anthropic-canonical vocabulary, acceptable as concrete example; IL-specific paths are not)

**Corollary:** The extractor cannot mechanically copy `source_finding.applicability` into `context.applies_to`. Source-finding `applicability` is IL-internal bookkeeping (which of *our* systems to attend to). ContextSpec `applies_to` is consumer-facing description (what kind of consumer situation fits). These are distinct layers. The extractor re-derives `applies_to` based on what the finding actually describes, in universal vocabulary.

## Why

Three forces converge:

1. **KB purpose.** The KB's goal is high-quality, research-backed resources consumable by people with very different needs and systems. Without per-artifact consumer-fit context, consumers cannot determine whether an artifact fits their situation.
2. **Deploy-boundary architecture.** The Librarian composes context at query time for IL-internal work — this is by design. Once an artifact deploys to a consumer system, the Librarian is not in the loop and must not be. The artifact must stand alone. ContextSpec is the interface that makes standalone viability possible.
3. **ContractSpec is insufficient alone.** DD-78's four sub-blocks capture *runtime* context (what must be true when the artifact fires, what happens when it breaks). They do not capture *consumer-fit* context (who should adopt this, at what stage, with what confidence, at what cost). These are complementary layers.

## Rules for `/extract-artifacts`

1. Every artifact written to `extracts/` MUST include a ContextSpec block. An artifact without ContextSpec is flagged and not written, same enforcement as missing ContractSpec.
2. The extractor re-derives `applies_to` in universal vocabulary. It does NOT mechanically copy `source_finding.applicability`.
3. `evidence_strength` and `adoption.status` inherit from source finding fields of the same name (these are already universal).
4. `adoption.notes` is optional; populate when the extractor has first-hand or source-documented past-signal worth preserving.
5. `stage`, `platform_coupling`, `autonomy`, `reversibility`, `auditability` are author-derived per artifact.

## Rules for Deployment

ContextSpec travels with the artifact across the deploy boundary. A deployed artifact in `meta-system/knowledge/`, `.claude/rules/`, or any consumer-system location MUST retain its ContextSpec. IL classification meta (`confidence`, `tier`, `reason_codes`, `co_occurrence`) do NOT travel — they are IL-internal and stripped at or before deployment.

## Scope and Non-Goals

- **In scope:** All artifact forms — rule, pattern, template, skill, agent. Same schema across forms (per DD-78's universality principle applied to ContextSpec).
- **Out of scope for v1:** Form-specific ContextSpec extensions (e.g., templates getting a `variables_required` field). Add extensions only when real needs surface from extracts.
- **Out of scope:** Automated track-record for past-signal (option 4 in the design discussion). `adoption.notes` captures self-reported past-signal for v1; structured track-record is deferred.
