# DD-123

> **For agents:** G4 `building-agent-evaluation-suites` is split into **G4a `verifying-agent-output`** (verification architecture — how to prove the output works) and **G4b `eval-driven-improvement-loops`** (using evals to drive autonomous agent/skill improvement). The 77 findings route A=60 (G4a), B=14 (G4b), shared=3 (both). The source guide is deprecated and lives at `archive/guides/building-agent-evaluation-suites.md`; new Evaluation findings route to G4a/G4b, never the deprecated source. This is the per-split substantive ruling; DD-98 is the procedural parent.

## The Decision

Guide **G4 — "Building Agent Evaluation Suites"** (`extracts/guides/building-agent-evaluation-suites.md`) crossed the DD-98 split trigger on two consecutive `/synthesize-guide` regens: the 2026-07-16 firing at 73 findings (session 147) and the 2026-07-19 firing at 77 findings (session 152), both with Codifier recommendation *proceed with split as proposed*. This DD codifies the executed split.

## Why

**Second consecutive proceed with a strengthening signal.** The trigger fired at 73 findings (2026-07-16) and again at 77 (2026-07-19); the 4 wave-4 findings split 3:1 toward the verification half, reinforcing rather than blurring the seam. The source is the corpus's largest guide (77 findings, ~1300 lines). Both destinations clear a healthy viability floor: G4a has 60 clean findings, G4b has 14 clean.

**Two distinct reader intents.** "Prove this agent's output actually works" (assertions, metrics, verification architectures, benchmark discipline, production evaluation, deploy gating) is a different question from "use evals to make the agent and its skills better over time" (keep/revert loops, convergence contracts, skill lifecycle evaluation, description optimization, with/without baselines, adopt-only-with-evidence). The source carried the improvement-loop material as two overloaded steps (Step 8 / Step 8b) that become G4b's spine; the two questions have different readers (suite designer vs. loop builder).

**Shared substrate is duplicated deliberately, not contested.** The 3 shared findings (capability/regression lifecycle, generator-assessor separation, class-aware rubric carve-outs) are the invariants both questions stand on; each destination needs them in place, so they appear in both `source_findings[]`. This is the normal ~4% shared tax, not a routing failure (0 contested).

## Rules

1. New Evaluation findings route to G4a or G4b per the trigger-keyword partition, never to the deprecated source (DD-98 acceptance criterion: deprecated guides do not accept new findings).
2. The source guide body is retained intact in `archive/guides/`; it is read-only history, not a live substrate surface. The Librarian composes G4a/G4b, not the archived source.
3. Shared findings' `consumed_by` lists BOTH destination filenames. A-only and B-only findings list exactly one.
4. If a future regen of either destination crosses the DD-98 trigger again, the split procedure recurses per DD-98 — G4a/G4b are ordinary guides subject to the same lifecycle.
