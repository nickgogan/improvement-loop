# DD-45

> **Superseded by DD-103 (2026-06-18).** There is no separate "active knowledge layer" above the engine — the knowledge vault now lives *inside* the engine (`systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/`) and knowledge is the engine's own product. The meta-system shell dissolved.

> **For agents:** DD-45 defines MetaSystem's role as the active knowledge layer that all systems draw from. When designing new components, agents, or workflows, check MetaSystem for applicable patterns and guidelines before proceeding.

> **Note:** This DD supersedes DD-43, which established MetaSystem as a rename of System Documentation. DD-45 broadens MetaSystem's role from governance container to active knowledge layer.

---

## Core Decision

MetaSystem is elevated from a governance container (holding shared values, principles, vocabulary, and the DD/IB databases) to an **active knowledge layer** that captures and codifies cross-system patterns. Systems don't just read governance rules from MetaSystem — they draw on its pattern library to inform what they build and how.

---

## What MetaSystem Contains

| Content Type | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Governance** | Values, principles, vocabulary, constitution | shared/values.md, Design Decision Guidelines |
| **System Patterns** | How to create, operate, and evolve systems | Agent design patterns, skill authoring guidelines |
| **Component Guidelines** | When to use agents vs skills vs plugins | Decision matrix for choosing the right abstraction |
| **Cross-System Patterns** | Patterns that apply across S1, S2, S3, IL | Error handling patterns, review gate patterns |
| **Research Distillations** | Codified findings from the Research Loop | Best practices derived from research findings |

---

## How MetaSystem Gets Updated

1. **Research Loop** produces findings (stored in Research Findings DB)
2. **Improvement Loop** evaluates findings and produces proposals
3. Approved proposals become **codified patterns** in MetaSystem
4. Nick may also directly author patterns based on operational observation

---

## How Systems Consume MetaSystem

Each system's architect and product agents draw from MetaSystem's pattern library to:

- Identify applicable patterns for new work
- Generate backlog items informed by meta-level guidance
- Mix meta-derived items with system-specific feature requests, bugs, and operational needs

MetaSystem does not push work to systems. Systems **pull** what's relevant.

---

## Relationship to Existing Governance

MetaSystem's governance content (values, principles, vocabulary, constitution) remains unchanged. This DD adds a **second dimension** — the knowledge and pattern layer — alongside the existing governance layer.
