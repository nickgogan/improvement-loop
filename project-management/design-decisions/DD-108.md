# DD-108

> **For agents:** The Owner *files* DDs and applies *authorized* supersessions — that is mechanics,
> done autonomously. Nick gates the **content** of a load-bearing decision, not the clerical act of
> recording it. Inline with Nick → gate conversationally, write the DD directly. Without Nick →
> stage in `governance/proposals/` for his later gate. Never silently edit a ratified DD (DD-44).
> The Owner cannot raise its own autonomy tiers.

---

## Core Decision

The Owner constitution (`agents/owner/agent.md` and the workspace subagent `.claude/agents/owner.md`)
stated "NEVER create or modify Design Decisions — propose them; Nick creates them." That no longer
matches how the system runs: the Owner has been filing DDs and applying amendments as a routine
mechanic, with Nick gating the *content* of each decision. The constitution and the lived practice
diverged — a drift the session-122 system audit surfaced.

This DD resolves the divergence in favour of the practice. The governing distinction is
**mechanics vs. content**:

- **Mechanics (Owner, autonomous):** authoring/filing a DD file, applying an authorized supersession
  per DD-44, updating inventories and cross-references, fixing drift. These are clerical acts of
  recording and maintenance.
- **Content (Nick gates):** the substance of a load-bearing decision — a new constraint, a
  vocabulary or boundary change, an autonomy change. Nick approves the *decision*, conversationally
  when he is inline, or via a `governance/proposals/` draft when he is not.

## What this preserves

- **DD-29 (human gate)** is intact. Nick still gates decision content and oversees live changes;
  there is no autonomous modification of consumer systems. What narrows is *where* the gate sits:
  on content, not on the mechanical recording.
- **DD-44 (immutability/supersession).** Ratified DDs are never silently edited. The Owner may file
  a new DD or apply a Nick-authorized supersession, never an in-place rewrite of a binding DD.
- **No self-promotion of autonomy.** The Owner cannot raise its own tiers — tier changes remain
  Human-Required.

## Why

The engine's trajectory is **supervised autonomy**: a system that runs on inputs (URLs to process,
end-user queries to help with) under Nick's oversight, rather than one that stops for approval on
every clerical step. Forcing Nick to be the hand that creates each DD file makes him a mechanical
bottleneck without adding a real gate — the gate that matters is on the decision's content, and that
he keeps. Moving the mechanics to the Owner is a concrete step toward extricating Nick from the
loop's clerical work while preserving his authority over what the system decides
(reduce-the-bottleneck principle; AI executes, Nick gates content).
