# DD-103

> **For agents:** There is one system now — the engine at `systems/improvement-loop/`. There is no separate "meta-system" governance/knowledge layer above it; that shell dissolved into the engine. Vision, values, and trajectory signals live in `CHARTER.md` at the workspace root (read it before architectural work). Engine operating rules live in `systems/improvement-loop/governance/`; design-wisdom (fractal pattern, DBDO pipeline, vocabulary) is plain knowledge in `systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/reference/`. Governance data (DDs, IB, System Log) routes to the engine's `project-management/` and `operations/` folders; the old "Cross-System" bucket is just the engine now. Household OS (→ Notion) and Claude Build (retired) are archived consumers, not peers.

---

## Context

The workspace was built as a three-system federation: a `meta-system` "governance + knowledge layer" governing peer systems (Household OS, Claude Build, Improvement Loop). That premise dissolved:

- **Household OS** is moving to Notion (custom agents over household data) — it leaves the markdown/fractal machinery and becomes a *consumer* the engine helps design, not a peer.
- **Claude Build** (a coding-agent harness) is retired — the coding-harness market is saturated; its opinionated-stack intent (Python / OpenAPI-first / SOA) becomes *knowledge artifacts* the engine can draw on, not a separate system.
- That left **Improvement Loop** as the only live system. "Meta-system governing peers" then governed one peer plus itself — overhead that fails the engine's own Rule 11 (abstractions must earn their keep).

This DD ratifies the collapse executed in the Phase-1 engine-collapse restructure (Steps P–8, session 117–119). It is one consolidating reset rather than a per-DD supersession sweep, per the locked plan's "DD-surgery weight" decision.

---

## The Reset

**Before:** `meta-system/` (governance source + knowledge vault + cross-system roadmap) governing three peer systems, each a fractal unit. Knowledge flowed research → meta-system patterns → per-system architect agents.

**After:** one workspace, one engine. The end-state has three altitudes inside the engine:

- **Bottom — research:** findings indexed by the dimension registry.
- **Middle — per-artifact:** Librarian concept docs (configuration axes) + `/assess-*` / `/design-*`.
- **Top — whole-system:** `/audit-system` (and the to-be-built `/design-harness`); schematics land here in Phase 2.

A small **charter** at the workspace root carries vision/mission/purpose/values/trajectory-signals (see DD-105). Operating rules stay in `CLAUDE.md` / `.claude/` / engine `governance/`. Design-wisdom is demoted to plain knowledge.

## Supersessions

### DD-32 — Four/three-system decomposition of Household OS → Superseded

The builder-vs-operator separation and interface contracts were premised on multiple live systems (S1/S2/S3/IL → Household OS / Claude Build / IL). With Household OS on Notion and Claude Build retired, there is one system. The decomposition no longer describes the workspace. Historical boundary principles are preserved in DD-32's frontmatter as a record; they no longer bind.

### DD-45 — MetaSystem as active knowledge layer → Superseded

DD-45 elevated `meta-system/` to a second dimension (knowledge + pattern layer) alongside governance, consumed by per-system agents. The knowledge vault now lives *inside* the engine (`systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/`, moved in Step 3); there is no separate layer to consume from. Knowledge is the engine's own product, stewarded by the engine Owner.

### DD-46 — Research-to-MetaSystem knowledge-flow pipeline → Superseded

The pipeline `Research → Improvement Loop → MetaSystem Patterns → Per-System Backlogs` assumed per-system architect/product agents pulling from a meta-layer. There are no peer systems and no per-system architect agents. The live pipeline is the engine's own intake → identify → extract → deploy flow (engine `CLAUDE.md` §The Pipeline, DD-80), terminating in an in-engine promotion to `knowledge/` — not a cross-system write.

## Amendments

### DD-50 — governance home

DD-50's clause "Active working copies of governance documents (values, principles, vocabulary, constitution) live in `meta-system/governance/` as the local-first authoritative source" is amended: those documents are retired. Their durable content was distilled into `CHARTER.md` (root) in Step 4; the originals (`constitution.md`, `values.md`) are archived at `archive/meta-system/governance/`. The authoritative vision/values source is now `CHARTER.md`; engine operating rules live in `systems/improvement-loop/governance/`. DD-50's research-database clause (IL-owned) is unchanged.

### DD-52 — fractal completeness + charter exception

DD-52's 7-folder fractal still governs the engine, which is now becoming fractal-complete (gaining `app/` in Step 1 and `knowledge/` in Step 3; `archive/` exists via `archive/` at root for retired work). Amendment: the **charter is a deliberate root-level exception** to the fractal — `CHARTER.md` sits at the workspace root, not inside any unit's `governance/`, because it scopes the whole workspace, not one unit. The example "`meta-system/`" in DD-52's body is now historical (that unit dissolved).

### DD-55 / DD-56 / DD-59 — Cross-System bucket folds into the engine

The three database-distribution DDs each defined a `Cross-System → systems/meta-system/...` routing row. That row folds into the engine: `target_system: Cross-System` and `target_system: Improvement Loop` both resolve to `systems/improvement-loop/` (DDs → `project-management/design-decisions/`, IB → `project-management/implementation-backlog/`, SL → `operations/system-log/`). The meta-system PM data was merged into the engine in Step 5 with no number collisions. The boundary-isolation rationale (system-scoped agents see only their own governance) is moot with one live system. **Archived systems keep their historical folders** — `archive/household-os/`, `archive/claude-build/`, and `archive/meta-system/` retain their `project-management/` and `operations/` databases as read-only history; DD/IB/SL numbers remain globally unique and are never reused.

## The A–G roadmap is superseded

The cross-system capability roadmap (steps A–G, building toward two-system MetaSystem-level capabilities) is superseded by the single-engine three-altitude architecture. `/audit-system` (roadmap step F/G output) survives as engine top-altitude capability; `/design-harness` survives as a future capability. The remaining two-system roadmap framing is retired. Re-sequencing the engine's `PROGRESS.md` priority queue is deferred to a post-Phase-1 session (handled by `/session-handoff`, not this DD).

---

## Why

- **Rule 11.** A meta-layer governing one live peer plus itself is an abstraction that stopped earning its keep the moment the federation went to one system.
- **Coherence.** Knowledge living "above" the engine while the only consumer *is* the engine created a cross-system-write fiction (the boundary-rules invariant) that had to be rewritten as an in-engine promotion. One engine removes the fiction.
- **Self-evolution.** Validated against a 2026 agent-taxonomy review: a layered model with a first-class evaluation/feedback layer is load-bearing for a self-evolving engine. The three-altitude shape (research → per-artifact → whole-system) is that layered model.
