# DD-32

> **Superseded by DD-103 (2026-06-18).** The multi-system decomposition no longer describes the workspace — the federation collapsed into one engine; Household OS moved to Notion and Claude Build was retired (DD-106). Historical boundary principles preserved below as a record; they no longer bind.

[Updated 2026-04-05: S1+S2 merged into Household OS per DD-58]

The original four-system decomposition (S1, S2, S3, S4) has been superseded by a three-system model:
- **Household OS** (formerly S1 + S2) — schema, operations, agents, workflows
- **Claude Build** (formerly S3) — vault architecture, schema modifications
- **Improvement Loop** — unchanged

The original decision text is preserved in frontmatter as a historical record. The boundary principles (builder vs. operator separation, interface contracts) remain valid under the new naming.
