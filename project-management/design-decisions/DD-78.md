# DD-78

> **For agents:** Every codified artifact gets a ContractSpec. No exceptions, no form-specific variants. Use the four sub-blocks: preconditions, invariants, governance, recovery.

---

## The Constraint

The ContractSpec shape applies universally across all five forms:

```yaml
contract:
  preconditions:    # What must be true before this artifact is used
  invariants:       # What must remain true while this artifact is active
  governance:       # Who owns, who can modify, what gates apply
  recovery:         # What happens when preconditions or invariants are violated
```

Form-specific details live in the artifact body, not in the ContractSpec. The ContractSpec captures the cross-cutting concerns that every artifact shares: when is it safe to use, what must hold, who controls it, and what breaks.

## Why

Validated across all five paper exercises in session 21 (pattern, rule, template, skill, agent). The four sub-blocks held without modification across all forms. No form needed a different shape or additional fields. The ABC framework (arxiv 2602.22302) provides the academic grounding; the exercises provided empirical validation.

The universal layer means downstream consumers (system owners, Form Architects, validation tools) can process ContractSpec uniformly without form-specific handling.

## Contract Triple-Role (amended 2026-04-26 per session-70 ratification)

Operational use of Contract during the Librarian reference-layer build (sessions 47–49) revealed that Contract sections do three distinct jobs. DD-78's original framing captured role 1; roles 2 and 3 emerged from the consumer-side audit pathway.

1. **Artifact-self-governance.** The original DD-78 role. The four sub-blocks govern the artifact's runtime behavior: what must hold before it fires (preconditions), what it preserves while active (invariants), who controls it (governance), what happens when it breaks (recovery). This is the producer-side semantic.

2. **Emergent audit criteria.** Consumer-side audit tooling (`/assess-skill`, `/assess-agent`) derives WHAT TO CHECK directly from Contract content. The same `invariants` field that governs runtime defines the audit checks. No separate audit-criteria schema is needed — Contract IS the audit criteria.

3. **Audit-applicability gating.** Contract `preconditions` act as gates determining WHETHER audit checks fire on a given artifact. The "Preconditions-as-gates pattern" (operations/references/librarian/audit.md) is the operational form: an audit check whose precondition is unmet is skipped, not failed.

The triple-role property means Contract is the universal interface between producers (the artifact) and consumers (runtime + auditors). Producers write Contract once; runtime, audit-criteria derivation, and audit-applicability gating all read the same fields.
