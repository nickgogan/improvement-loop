# DD-78

> **For agents:** Every codified artifact gets a ContractSpec. No exceptions, no form-specific variants. Use the four sub-blocks: preconditions, invariants, governance, recovery.

---

## The Constraint

The ContractSpec shape applies universally across all five forms:

```yaml
contract:
  preconditions:    # What must be true before this artifact is used
  invariants:       # What must remain true while this artifact is active
  governance:       # Who owns, who can modify, what gates apply
  recovery:         # What happens when preconditions or invariants are violated
```

Form-specific details live in the artifact body, not in the ContractSpec. The ContractSpec captures the cross-cutting concerns that every artifact shares: when is it safe to use, what must hold, who controls it, and what breaks.

## Why

Validated across all five paper exercises in session 21 (pattern, rule, template, skill, agent). The four sub-blocks held without modification across all forms. No form needed a different shape or additional fields. The ABC framework (arxiv 2602.22302) provides the academic grounding; the exercises provided empirical validation.

The universal layer means downstream consumers (system owners, Form Architects, validation tools) can process ContractSpec uniformly without form-specific handling.
