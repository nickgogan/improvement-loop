# DD-111

> **For agents:** `extracts/` is **not** a single drafts-staging bin. `extracts/guides/` and
> `extracts/patterns/` are **live substrate** — the research-substrate library the Librarian composes.
> Only `extracts/{rules,skills,templates,agents}/` are "staging residue" (un-promoted drafts). No files
> moved (rename-in-place, option 1a); the framing lives in `extracts/CLAUDE.md` and the pipeline guide.

---

## Core Decision

The DD-80 pipeline frames `extracts/` as a staging area ("stage before deploying," DD-39/DD-80) and
`knowledge/` as the deployed destination. In practice the staging→deploy flow barely ran, yet
`extracts/` is heavily populated — and `extracts/guides/` became the Librarian's **live Tier-1
substrate**. The "staging" label stopped describing what `extracts/` is.

The session-126 reconciliation audit
(`project-management/design-notes/2026-06-20-extracts-knowledge-reconciliation.md`) measured this:

| Subtree | Access pattern | Status |
|---|---|---|
| `extracts/guides/` | slug-referenced from concept docs, guide-routing-table, skills (100%) | **Tier 1 — live substrate.** "Staging" is simply wrong here. |
| `extracts/patterns/` | category/tag/dimension browse; ~10% slug-pinned | **Tier 2 — live corpus.** Un-pinned but reachable. |
| `extracts/{rules,skills,templates,agents}/` | ~5% slug-pinned; not composed as substrate | **Staging residue.** Un-promoted drafts. |

The two-bodies frame: `extracts/` = **research-substrate library** (external agentic-coding
best-practice the Librarian composes, consumed pull-style); `knowledge/` = the **engine's
self-knowledge** (how this engine is built/operates). They share folder names but are different bodies.

## The decision — rename in place (1a)

- **No files move.** ~15 live substrate pointers stay valid (zero churn).
- The framing change is documented in the two consuming docs: `extracts/CLAUDE.md` (reframed from
  "Staging Area" to the two-bodies/three-tiers model) and
  `knowledge/guides/research-to-codification-pipeline.md` (Current State + extracts/ description).
- `extracts/guides/` + `extracts/patterns/` are documented as the Librarian's substrate library; only
  `rules/skills/templates/agents` are "staging."

## Relationship to prior DDs

- **Amends DD-39 / DD-80 (does not supersede).** Stage-before-deploy still governs the four residue
  subtrees (`rules/skills/templates/agents`). What changes is that `guides/` and `patterns/` are
  reclassified as live substrate rather than deploy-pending drafts. DD-39 and DD-80 remain Binding.
- **DD-103** (single-engine collapse) supplies the two-bodies vocabulary (the Librarian composes
  external substrate; the engine keeps its own self-knowledge).

## Why 1a over 1b (relocate)

Relocating `guides/` + `patterns/` to a first-class home (`knowledge/substrate/` or top-level
`substrate/`) would give a cleaner conceptual map but rewrites ~15 live pointers across concept docs,
skills, and the guide-routing-table — a spec'd migration of its own. **Rule 11:** resolve the actual
incoherence (substrate mislabeled as staging) at near-zero risk now; reserve the relocation for a
dedicated migration only if the folder name keeps causing confusion (wait for recurrence before paying
the move cost).

## What this does NOT change

- It does **not** move any files.
- It does **not** weaken the human gate (DD-29) or the staging discipline for the residue subtrees.
- It does **not** merge `knowledge/patterns/` (curated engine self-knowledge) with
  `extracts/patterns/` (research substrate) — they stay separate, cross-linked (different roles).
- The staging-residue promote-or-prune pass is a **separate** operation (tracked alongside IB-170 /
  this session's task 2b), not part of this framing decision.
