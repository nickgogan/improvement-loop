# DD-29

> **For agents:** DD-29 is the autonomy boundary for the Improvement Loop. No stage of the pipeline may autonomously modify any live system (S2 config, S3 vault, Perplexity skills). Every transition between pipeline stages requires human approval.

---

## Core Constraint

The Improvement Loop pipeline is human-gated at every stage boundary:

**Research** -> [Human Gate] -> **Propose** -> [Human Gate] -> **Evaluate** -> **Enhance** -> [Human Gate] -> **Deploy**

No autonomous modification of any live system. The pipeline produces recommendations; Nick makes deployment decisions.

---

## Gate Definitions

| Gate | Between | What Nick Reviews | Approval Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|
| Gate 1 | Research -> Propose | Delta report, new findings | Are findings relevant? |
| Gate 2 | Propose -> Evaluate | Proposals with conflict analysis | Are proposals worth evaluating? |
| Gate 3 | Enhance -> Deploy | Final rewrites with diffs | Is the rewrite correct and safe? |

---

## Future Relaxation Criteria

Gates may be relaxed when:
- 5+ successful end-to-end runs
- >90% approval rate for two-way door proposals
- Rollback mechanism exists
