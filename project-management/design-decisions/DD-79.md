# DD-79

> **For agents:** When creating or updating findings destined for the Router, populate all four fields. Write `"none"` explicitly if a field is empty — do not omit the field.

---

## The Constraint

Four fields are required on every ResearchFinding that enters the Form Router pipeline:

| Field | Purpose | Empty value |
|---|---|---|
| `assumptions` | What the finding takes for granted that may not hold in all contexts | `"none"` |
| `scope_constraints` | Boundaries within which the finding is valid | `"none"` |
| `excluded_evidence` | Evidence considered but not included, and why | `"none"` |
| `dissenting_findings` | Related findings that contradict or qualify this one | `"none"` |

### Rules

- All four fields are **required**, not optional. `"none"` is the explicit empty value.
- Fields must never be omitted from frontmatter — omission is ambiguous (was it considered and empty, or never evaluated?).
- These fields feed into the Form Architect's ContractSpec generation (DD-78), specifically the `preconditions` and `governance` sub-blocks.
- The Form Router may use `dissenting_findings` as an escalation signal (rubric open question #5).

## Why

Grounded in SEI ML Mismatch Descriptors (assumptions/scope) and GRADE EtD dissent fields (excluded evidence/dissenting findings). Validated in all four session 21 exercises — all four populated at least 2 of 4 fields non-trivially.

The failure mode these fields prevent: tacit knowledge loss at the finding-to-artifact boundary. Without explicit assumptions, a pattern codified from a finding inherits unstated constraints that downstream consumers cannot see. Without excluded evidence, the artifact appears more universally applicable than the evidence supports.
