# DD-30

> **For agents:** DD-30 defines how IL agents are organized. If you are executing an IL skill, this DD tells you your role boundaries, what you can read/write, and how your cognitive disposition differs from other IL agents.

---

## Core Decision

| Phase | Skill | Cognitive Disposition | Write Domain |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research | research-loop | Skeptical, evidence-focused, neutral | Research Sources, Findings, Authorities DBs |
| Proposal | research-proposer | Pragmatic, system-aware, opinionated | Improvement Proposals DB |

---

## Strict Read/Write Boundaries

The Researcher writes ONLY to Research Sources, Findings, and Authorities. The Proposer writes ONLY to Improvement Proposals. Neither crosses into the other's write domain.
