# DD-104

> **For agents:** The engine has three altitudes. When you work, know which one you're in. **Bottom (research):** `research-findings/`, indexed by `operations/references/research-dimensions.md`. **Middle (per-artifact):** the Librarian concept docs in `operations/references/librarian/{agent,skill,prompt,harness,memory}.md` are the configuration axes; `/assess-*` audits an artifact, `/design-*` authors one (Rule 12 — the same concept doc drives both). **Top (whole-system):** `/audit-system` composes the middle assessors over a whole folder; `/design-harness` (future) and the schematic library (Phase 2) live here. Each altitude is built on the one below — top composes middle, middle is grounded in bottom.

---

## Core Decision

After the federation collapse (DD-103), the engine is the sole system. Its internal structure is three altitudes, not a flat pile of skills:

| Altitude | Scope | Substrate | Skills |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Bottom** | Research / evidence | `research-findings/`, `research-sources/`, `research-authorities/`, `watched-libraries/`; indexed by the dimension registry (incl. Dim 7 Evaluation, Dim 9 Governance) | `/research-loop`, `/identify-artifacts`, `/extract-artifacts`, `/ask-kb` |
| **Middle** | Per-artifact (one skill, one agent, one prompt) | Librarian concept docs = the configuration axes (agent / skill / prompt / harness / memory) | `/assess-skill`, `/assess-agent`, `/assess-prompt`; `/design-skill`, `/design-agent` |
| **Top** | Whole-system composition | The middle assessors, composed; the schematic library (Phase 2) | `/audit-system`; `/design-harness` (future) |

**The altitudes nest.** The top composes the middle ( `/audit-system` dispatches `/assess-*` per artifact). The middle is grounded in the bottom (concept-doc criteria cite findings). This is the layered model validated against the 2026 agent-taxonomy review.

---

## Bilingual composition (Rule 12)

Each middle-altitude concept doc carries both a §Composition section (drives `/assess-*`, audit mode) and a §Construction section (drives `/design-*`, author mode). An abstraction carrying one but not the other is in debt. This is why assess/design come in pairs per axis.

---

## Fractal completeness and the charter exception

The engine follows the 7-folder fractal (DD-52) and is becoming fractal-complete: `app/` (relocated tools — transcript-fetcher, pdf-to-markdown), `knowledge/` (patterns, guides, templates, reference), `governance/`, `agents/`, `project-management/`, `operations/`, plus `archive/` at workspace root for retired work. The **charter is the deliberate exception** — it sits at the workspace root (`CHARTER.md`), not inside any unit, because it scopes the whole workspace (DD-103 §DD-52 amendment, DD-105).

---

## What this does NOT decide

- The **schematic form** and the evaluation/feedback layer are Phase 2 (deferred). DD-104 names the top altitude's future tenants but does not define the schematic shape.
- It does not change the research pipeline mechanics (DD-80) or the human gate (DD-29).
- It does not add new agents or skills — it describes how the existing ones stratify.

## Why

A flat catalog of ~40 skills hides the dependency structure that makes the engine self-evolving: research feeds per-artifact judgment, which composes into whole-system judgment. Naming the altitudes makes the "what's grounded in what" explicit, so a finding that moves (bottom) flags the concept docs (middle) and schematics (top) that depend on it — the drift loop the engine runs on.
