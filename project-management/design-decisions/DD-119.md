# DD-119

> **For agents:** Three old DDs carried living invariants wrapped in dead mechanism text (Notion-era pages, dissolved directories, pre-collapse team framing). This DD is the clean restatement; the three originals are Superseded. No behavior changes — if you were following DD-39/47/61 correctly post-collapse, you were already following this.

---

## The Three Invariants

### 1. Stage before deploy (from DD-39)

Every artifact bound for a live surface passes through a **staging body** before deployment, and crosses into its enforcement location only by **Nick's deployment act**. Today the staging body is `extracts/` (reframed by DD-111 as substrate, not a waiting room — the invariant survives the reframe: what changes a live system is always a gated promotion, never a direct write). Codification remains a distinct pipeline stage between approval and deployment.

*What died with DD-39's text:* the "Codified Practices page" (Notion-era) and the `research-codifier` skill (superseded by the DD-80 pipeline).

### 2. Workspace shape (from DD-47)

The workspace at root is:

| Directory | Role | PARA |
|-----------|------|------|
| `systems/` | Graduated infrastructure — the engine (`improvement-loop/`), sole live system post-DD-103 | Areas |
| `incubator/` | Staging for new projects | Projects |
| `archive/` | Retired units (Household OS, Claude Build, meta-system shell) | Archives |

`CHARTER.md` sits at root as the one deliberate fractal exception (DD-105). **No governance data lives at root** — DDs, IB items, and the (frozen) System Log are system-scoped per DD-55/DD-56/DD-59.

*What died with DD-47's text:* `meta-system/` (dissolved into the engine, DD-103), `s1-schema/` (left with Household OS, DD-106), root-level governance databases (distributed by DD-55/56/59), and the Improvement Loop as a root-level peer (it lives in `systems/`).

### 3. Milestone gates (from DD-61)

Agents hand off freely **within** a milestone; the human gate at **milestone completion** is mandatory and non-negotiable. This is the milestone-boundary application of the same rule DD-29 states for pipeline-stage boundaries — one human-gate invariant, two boundary types. Any future gate-tiering refinement (IB-173) amends DD-29 and this DD together.

## Why

The substrate audit (gate G2) found all three DDs kernel-shaped but textually dead: a downstream consumer pulling the governance kernel needs these invariants, and would currently receive them wrapped in references to structures that no longer exist. Restating once now means the Phase-5 kernel manifest can cite one clean DD instead of three stale ones with mental patches.
