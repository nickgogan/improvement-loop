# DD-40

> **For agents:** DD-40 establishes that Meta-System lives in the Household Teamspace alongside UB3, not in a separate teamspace.

---

[Updated 2026-04-05: S1+S2 merged into Household OS per DD-58]

## The Two Layers

| Layer | What It Is | Example Pages |
|---|---|---|
| **Operational/Runtime (UB3)** | Where humans and Household OS-scoped agents do work | Tasks, Projects, Goals, Notes, Quick Capture, My Day |
| **Design-Time (Meta-System)** | Where architecture, decisions, and governance live | Architecture, Implementation, Operations, Improvement Loop |

Both layers serve the household from the same teamspace. They are peers, not parent-child.

## When to Revisit

- If Meta-System grows to 100+ pages
- If Notion Custom Agents get teamspace-scoped permissions
- If a third household member joins
