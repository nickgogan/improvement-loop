# DD-75

> **For agents:** Override always drops out of autonomous tier. If the Router disagrees with the Proposer's candidate form, the classification goes to guided review — even at HIGH confidence.

---

## The Constraint

In the Form Router's tier dispatch logic:

| Condition | Tier |
|---|---|
| `router_confidence >= HIGH` AND `overridden = false` AND no scope-constraint conflicts | autonomous |
| **`overridden = true`** (any confidence) | **guided** |
| `MEDIUM <= router_confidence < HIGH` | guided |
| `router_confidence < MEDIUM` OR form ambiguous OR cross-artifact conflict | hitl |

The override check takes priority over confidence. A HIGH-confidence override is the worst-case silent failure if auto-promoted — it means the Router was confident about a classification that contradicts the Proposer's assessment.

## Why

Validated through 50-finding calibration set (session 22). The calibration showed only 1 override in 50 classifications (row 4: agent→pattern), but override detection is the Router's primary rubber-stamp prevention mechanism. Confident disagreement between pipeline stages is exactly what humans should see.

Supersedes the aiAgentsFocus report's softer treatment where `overridden: true` was a GUIDED trigger but not a hard rule.
