# DD-102

> **For agents:** Guide split trigger is now ≥45 findings AND ≥2 practitioner questions (raised from ≥25). Each proposed destination must have ≥12 cleanly-routed findings or the proposal is suppressed. Post-synthesis, if a guide exceeds 6000 words, surface an informational observation in the synthesis report. All other DD-98 mechanics (proposal format, Nick gate, per-split DD, changelog entries) remain unchanged.

---

## The Amendment

### 1. Finding-count threshold: 25 → 45

DD-98 §The Constraint item (1) is amended:

**Was:** "The guide's current `source_findings[]` contains **≥25 findings**."

**Now:** "The guide's current `source_findings[]` contains **≥45 findings**."

### 2. Sub-guide viability criterion (new)

DD-98 §The Constraint gains a third conjunctive requirement:

3. **Sub-guide viability.** Each proposed destination guide must receive **≥12 findings** that route cleanly (disposition `A` or `B`, not `shared` or `contested`). A split that produces a runt destination (<12 clean findings) is suppressed — the Codifier does NOT emit a proposal. Instead, it surfaces an inline observation: "G-`<stem>` at `<count>` findings with `<N>` practitioner questions; proposed split suppressed — destination `<name>` would receive only `<M>` clean findings (minimum: 12). Monitor."

This prevents fragmentation into thin guides that lack enough mass to be useful standalone. The threshold of 12 is calibrated against the corpus: the smallest viable guides (G1 at 11, G6 at 5) show that guides below ~10 findings tend to be early-stage stubs, not mature playbooks.

### 3. Post-synthesis word-count observation (new)

After Step 3 (guide draft), if the synthesized output exceeds **6000 words**, the synthesis report includes an informational observation:

> "Guide `<stem>` synthesized at `<N>` words (`<T>` estimated tokens). Consider whether the guide's scope should narrow on next regen."

This is **not a trigger** — it does not emit a split proposal and does not gate the write. It provides a consumer-cost signal that the finding-count threshold alone cannot capture (some findings synthesize into a paragraph; others into a full section). Nick reads the observation alongside any split proposals when deciding whether to act.

### 4. Single-condition observation text updates

DD-98's dispatch table for single-condition observations is updated to reflect the new threshold:

| Trigger result | Action |
|---|---|
| **All three conditions met** (≥45 findings AND ≥2 questions AND both destinations ≥12 clean) | Emit split-proposal file. Regen continues. |
| **Count ≥45, single question** | Inline: "G-`<stem>` at `<count>` findings; remains single-question. Monitor for question bifurcation on next regen." |
| **Count ≥45, ≥2 questions, runt destination** | Inline: "G-`<stem>` at `<count>` findings with `<N>` questions; split suppressed — destination `<name>` would receive only `<M>` clean findings (minimum: 12). Monitor." |
| **Question count ≥2, count <45** | Inline: "G-`<stem>` at `<count>` findings; touches `<N>` practitioner questions but below volume threshold (45). Monitor." |
| **Neither threshold met** | No-op. |

### 5. Acceptance criteria updates

DD-98's acceptance criteria table gains these rows:

| Test | Expected |
|---|---|
| Guide reaches ≥45 findings with ≥2 questions but one destination would have <12 clean findings | No proposal emitted. Inline observation surfaces the suppressed split with specific counts. |
| Guide synthesizes to >6000 words | Informational observation in synthesis report. No proposal emitted from word count alone. |
| Guide at 30 findings with ≥2 practitioner questions | No proposal emitted (below 45 threshold). Inline observation: below volume threshold. |

DD-98's existing acceptance criterion "Guide reaches ≥25 findings with a single coherent practitioner question" is updated to read "≥45 findings."

## Why

**The 25-finding threshold generates noise as the KB matures.** Since DD-98 was ratified (session 74), the corpus has grown from ~400 to ~717 findings. Four guides now exceed 25 findings (G2:64, G4:46, G3:42, G9:38, G10:37). Of the three split proposals emitted, the Codifier recommended deferral on two (G9, G3) — evidence that the threshold fires before the guides have accumulated enough structural strain to justify the split cost.

**Observed evidence for the new threshold.** G4 at 46 findings with a single practitioner question is the canonical example of a large, coherent guide. G2 at 64 findings is the first guide where split review was genuinely warranted. Setting the threshold at 45 means G4-scale guides don't trigger, while G2-scale guides do — matching the observed inflection point.

**Sub-guide viability prevents fragmentation.** A split that produces a 35/5 distribution is worse than a single 40-finding guide. The runt guide lacks depth, requires its own maintenance overhead (changelog, harvest queue, routing-table row), and creates a consumer navigation decision that isn't worth the cost. The ≥12 criterion ensures both destinations are substantial.

**Word count captures consumer cost that finding count misses.** Some findings contribute a single sentence to synthesis; others contribute an entire section with templates and examples. The word-count observation gives Nick a signal about the reader's experience that is orthogonal to the finding-count threshold.

**Long-running systems must tolerate growth.** The IL is a permanent system, not a project with a finish line. Guide finding counts will continue to rise. The split mechanism should fire when guides genuinely strain, not when they reach a healthy depth on a single topic.

## Rules for Codifier

1. DD-98's rules remain in force, with these modifications:
   - Rule 2: "Both thresholds, no shortcuts" becomes "All three conditions, no shortcuts" — count ≥45 AND questions ≥2 AND sub-guide viability (≥12 clean per destination).
   - After Step 3 draft: check word count. If >6000, surface observation in synthesis report. No other action.

2. **Retroactive effect on pending proposals.** The three pending split proposals (G2, G3, G9) are not invalidated — they were correctly emitted under the prior threshold. Nick's gate evaluates them on substance, not on whether the threshold that triggered them has since changed. Future regens of those guides will evaluate against the new thresholds.

3. **In-flight subagents.** If a subagent was briefed before DD-102 landed and emits a split proposal under the old ≥25 threshold, the proposal stands — the orchestrator notes the threshold change in the synthesis report and Nick evaluates accordingly.

## Scope

- **Amends:** DD-98 §The Constraint (finding-count threshold, dispatch table, acceptance criteria).
- **Adds:** Sub-guide viability criterion, post-synthesis word-count observation.
- **Does not change:** Practitioner-question threshold (still ≥2), proposal format, per-split DD requirement, execution procedure, DD-94 trigger-tag enum, preserved-section disposition rules.
- **Does not supersede DD-98.** DD-98 remains the procedural parent for guide splits. DD-102 tunes the numeric thresholds and adds qualifying criteria.
