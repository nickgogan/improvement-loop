# DD-115

> **For agents:** To orient on the system, read `systems/improvement-loop/governance/FOUNDATIONS.md` — the generated map of the ~20 spine DDs. It is a VIEW; the DD files are canonical (DD wins on any conflict). Do not edit FOUNDATIONS.md by hand. To add/remove a spine DD, set/clear `foundational: true` on the DD (testing it against the criteria below), then run `generate_foundations.py`. A pre-commit check enforces sync.

## Problem

Governance is distributed across CHARTER.md, two CLAUDE.md files, rule files, and ~80+ individual DD files. ~20 of those DDs define the system's spine; the rest are operational. Nothing distinguished them, so the backbone was discoverable only by reading or grepping the whole corpus — acute comprehension friction for both Nick and agents (a foundational infra decision, DD-84's publish model, was effectively un-findable despite being recorded). A council review (Perplexity, 3-lens) converged: yes to a spine layer, but it must be a *generated view over canonical DDs*, never a hand-authored second source of truth.

## Decision rationale

- **Generated, not authored.** A hand-written digest would rot and would violate the no-hardcoded-counts / no-per-session-maintenance principle. Deriving from frontmatter keeps the single source of truth in the DDs (maintenance lives where the decision lives).
- **DD immutability preserved.** `foundational` is non-semantic curation metadata; it does not touch the `decision` text. Adding it is mechanics, not a DD amendment.
- **Cache-coherence is structurally bounded.** DDs are immutable (superseded, not edited), so the only invalidation events are "a DD gains/loses the flag" or "a foundational DD is superseded" — both low-frequency and covered by the `status == Binding` filter plus the pre-commit staleness check.
- **Context budget.** The map is referenced from CLAUDE.md, not inlined, keeping always-on context lean (DD-74).
- **Anti-drift is the whole point.** Without explicit criteria, "foundational" creeps until the spine is the whole corpus. The C/X criteria + displacement cap make every future flag a tested decision.

## Scope of execution (session 128)

- Tagged 20 spine DDs `foundational: true`: DD-29, 36, 37, 41, 44, 47, 52, 53, 54, 55, 56, 59, 80, 82, 84, 86, 103, 104, 105, 108 (selected and ratified against the criteria with Nick).
