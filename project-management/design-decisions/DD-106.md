# DD-106

> **For agents:** Claude Build and Household OS are **archived, not live**. Do not treat them as session targets, peers, or active backlogs. Claude Build is retired outright (its intent lives on as knowledge). Household OS is moving to Notion — operational work happens there via Notion MCP, and the engine's role toward it is *consumer design help*, not co-development. Their archived folders (`archive/claude-build/`, `archive/household-os/`) are read-only history. Household OS's Notion schemas + architecture docs live in `systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/reference/` as design substrate for the Notion build.

---

## Core Decision

The federation collapse (DD-103) hinges on two systems leaving the workspace. This DD records why and how.

### Claude Build — retired

Claude Build was a coding-agent harness (vault architecture, schema modifications, opinionated stack). The coding-harness market is saturated — GSD, BMAD, OpenSpec, Antigravity, and Claude Code itself already occupy the space. Reinventing it is low-value. **Decision:** retire it. Its opinionated-stack intent (Python / OpenAPI-first / SOA) is preserved as *knowledge artifacts* the engine can draw on when designing coding workcells, not as a separate live system. Archived at `archive/claude-build/` with its DD/IB/SL history intact.

### Household OS — moves to Notion

Household OS was the operational system (schema, operations, agents, workflows over household data). It moves to **Notion** — custom Notion agents over household data — leaving the markdown/fractal machinery behind. **Decision:** archive the fractal unit; Household OS becomes a *consumer* the engine helps design (a demand profile for a future schematic), not a peer that co-evolves in this workspace. Archived at `archive/household-os/`. Its **7 Notion schemas + architecture docs were lifted into `systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/reference/`** (Step 2) as design substrate for the Notion build.

---

## Consequences

- **Notion MCP** is for Household OS *operational* work only — never for engine governance data (unchanged from prior rule; reaffirmed here now that Household OS is Notion-resident).
- **No automated feedback loop** between the archived systems and the engine. If the engine produces design help for the Notion Household OS, Nick carries it across — there is no live cross-system pipeline (the per-system-architect pipeline was superseded in DD-103 via DD-46).
- **Governance history is preserved.** Archived DD/IB/SL numbers remain globally unique and are never reused; new engine entries continue the global sequence.

## Why

A system the engine no longer co-develops should not sit in the engine's live tree advertising itself as a peer — that is exactly the unearned abstraction Rule 11 forbids. Archiving with intact history keeps the record without the overhead. Preserving Claude Build's stack intent and Household OS's schemas as *knowledge* means the engine loses nothing it can use, while shedding the maintenance of two dormant fractal units.
