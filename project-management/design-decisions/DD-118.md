## Why

Three supersession events left strandees behind: DD-103/DD-106 obsoleted the five Notion/Household-era structure DDs without formally superseding them; DD-80 stranded DD-31; DD-116 stranded DD-90. Each dead DD still read `status: Binding`, so the active set (DD-44: filter on Binding) overstated the operative rulebook. One consolidating DD closes all seven at minimum ceremony — the substrate audit's DD-class finding (gate G1), sanctioned by Nick under the session-139 door-type delegation.

## Non-Goals

- The already-Superseded DDs flagged for archival placement in the same audit (DD-30/32/35/43/45/46/48/65/72) are untouched by this DD; physical placement is a separate, cheaper question deferred until any move is actually worth it.
