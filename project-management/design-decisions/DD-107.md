# DD-107

> **For agents:** The **schematic** is the engine's top-altitude artifact form (the tenant DD-104 deferred). It maps *demand → configuration* and lives in `knowledge/schematics/`. Author one by capturing a **recurring cluster** against `knowledge/templates/schematic-template.md` — not by extracting a single finding (schematics are **curated, not pipeline-extracted**; do not route them through `/identify-artifacts` or `/extract-artifacts`). Every schematic MUST carry an **Evaluation & feedback** section and `grounded_in` links that resolve to real findings — without those it is not self-evolving and not done. Tag `altitude: middle` for a single-agent config, `top` for a composition.

---

## Core Decision

Phase 1 (DD-103/104) collapsed the federation into one engine with three altitudes and named
the schematic as the top altitude's content — but explicitly deferred defining it. This DD
defines it.

A **schematic** is an evidence-grounded `demand → configuration` blueprint. Its body is fixed
(the form contract in `knowledge/templates/schematic-template.md`):

1. **Demand** — function (coding / research / operations / assistant / audit) × scope/lifespan
   (personal-persistent ↔ project-bound) × non-functionals (data boundary, reliability,
   autonomy need, cost).
2. **Configuration**, layered by volatility (stable core first): **capability + memory core**
   (reusable across schematics) → **coordination / architecture** → **autonomy** (5-level ladder:
   operator → collaborator → consultant → approver → observer) → **deployment surface**
   (Claude Code / local+local-LLM / provider-API / Notion-embedded).
3. **Evaluation & feedback** — **required**. How it's evaluated (outcome vs process; independent
   vs self-report) and its feedback mechanism (internal / external / multi-agent / human). This
   layer is what makes a configuration self-evolving rather than a static recipe.
4. **Grounding & composition** — `grounded_in` (findings) + `composed_of` (artifacts/templates).
5. **Risk & maturity** — light risk note; `seed` vs `validated`; revisit trigger.

**Home:** `systems/improvement-loop/knowledge/schematics/`, with a Dataview-driven `_index.md`.

**Curated, not extracted.** Schematics are populated by capturing recurring named clusters, not
by walking the axis cross-product (the design space is sparse). They are therefore **out of scope
for the identify/extract Form Router** — a deliberate boundary, not an omission.

**Schema:** `_schema.yaml` gains `schematic` (type CV), `system-design` (category CV), and the
schematic-only fields `altitude`, `maturity`, `grounded_in`, `composed_of`.

## What this does NOT decide

- It does **not** bring schematics into `/detect-drift` scope — that earns its keep once
  schematics exist (next slice; the plan's Phase 2 item 2).
- It does **not** add the execution-surface Librarian axis or Builder-mode demand→schematic
  matching — deferred as demand-gated (Rule 11); seeds express deployment surface inline.
- It does **not** change the research pipeline (DD-80) or the human gate (DD-29).

## Why

DD-104 named the top altitude but left it empty; the Charter makes the eval/feedback layer the
load-bearing thing that distinguishes a self-evolving configuration from a recipe. Fixing the
form now — grounded in two clusters the engine actually operates (the research-loop and
`/audit-system`) — gives the top altitude real content while keeping the surface minimal
(Charter: "ship minimum viable; complexity is earned through use"). Curated-not-extracted keeps
the form honest to how the design space actually populates (sparse clusters), avoiding a
combinatorial library no one asked for (Rule 11).
