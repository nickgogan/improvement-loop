# DD-124

> **For agents:** The engine's machine-readable self-description starts with
> `governance/system-contract.yaml` — root descriptor + ten wiring rows, all five
> `required` tiers confirmed (CLAUDE.md injection, human gate, named skills,
> commit gate, git). Per-asset records are specced but NOT written until E3's
> build; do not create them early. The YAML is hand-declared v0 — treat it as
> gated content (edit only through a Nick-ruled change), not a generated artifact
> yet. The design note (2026-07-19, `stage: stable`) carries the full ruled schema
> including the deferred Layer-2 record shape.

---

## The Ruling

Interview 2026-07-22 (question-by-question, recorded in the design note §7):

1. **Stance** — discover-then-declare hybrid accepted *at wiring-rows scope*:
   ship Layer 3 (wiring rows) + a minimal Layer-1 root now; defer Layer-2
   per-asset records to E3's build. The record schema stays ruled in the design
   note so E3 instantiates, not redesigns.
2. **Tier table** — all five `required` rows stand: `always-on-entry`,
   `human-approval-channel`, `skill-registry`, `commit-gate`, `session-spine`.
   Nick's ruling keeps commit-gate a hosting precondition despite bypassability —
   mechanical governance enforcement is non-negotiable. `memory-store` stays
   provisionally `optional` pending E1.
3. **Home** — `governance/`: the contract is kernel-shaped (what any harness must
   provide) and part of the export unit E4/E5 consume.
4. **Naming** — `system-contract.yaml`: survives the open engine-rename question
   and matches the KB pattern vocabulary
   (`machine-readable-system-contract-with-wiring-rows`).
5. **Deferred to E3 build** — Layer-2 source of truth (record-as-canonical vs
   frontmatter extension vs sidecar), pointer-only vs self-contained records,
   the agent extension field set, workspace-root vs engine-scoped ownership, and
   v1 instance scope.

## Why

Queue item 1 (Nick, 2026-07-18) asked to *begin* the language, not discharge E3.
The wiring rows are the smallest port-critical piece with near-zero maintenance
(they change only when the engine starts depending on something new); per-asset
records before a compiler exists are a hand-maintained corpus without a consumer —
the minimum-viable-abstraction rule routes them to E3, their first real consumer.
The adversarial-review amendments (2026-07-20) and the KB grounding (wiring-row
schema template, Eve counter-stance, three-section canon) are in the design note.

## Consequences

- `governance/system-contract.yaml` exists and is Nick-gated content.
- E3's build inherits a ruled schema + five open questions, re-asked then.
- E4 reads the `required` set as its enforcement floor; E5's compiler (v2) takes
  over inventory generation and adds the drift-hash manifest.
