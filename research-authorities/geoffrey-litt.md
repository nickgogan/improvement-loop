  personal-computing vision — a researcher's lineage applied to production
  AI-assisted engineering. ExplainDiff gist and companion essay are public; Notion
  shipped "coding agents live in Notion pages" the week of the talk, corroborating the
  shared-spaces claim as production practice, not concept demo.
