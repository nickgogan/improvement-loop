## Why We Watch

Tier 1 authority — research publications reveal model-level capabilities and constraints that change how we should design agents, structure prompts, and evaluate outputs. Only relevant when findings are actionable: a research insight that doesn't change how we build is not a finding.

## Relevance Filter

The test: **does this research change how MetaSystem should design, prompt, orchestrate, or evaluate agents?**

**Include (maps to research dimensions):**
- Model capability discoveries that change agent design assumptions — tool use behaviors, reasoning depth, context utilization patterns (Dim 2, Dim 3)
- Evaluation methodology applicable to agent output verification (Dim 7)
- Multi-step reasoning or planning insights relevant to orchestration design (Dim 6)
- Extended thinking, function calling internals, or context handling that informs prompt architecture (Dim 1, Dim 3)
- Governance-relevant research — autonomy boundaries, oversight effectiveness, delegation patterns (Dim 9)

**Skip:** Pure interpretability research without agent design implications, alignment/safety theory without actionable mechanisms, model cards or benchmark tables without "so what" for agent builders, policy papers, scaling law studies unless they change cost/performance assumptions for our model tier choices.
