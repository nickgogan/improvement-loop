# Improvement Loop — References

Durable reference material for how the Improvement Loop system operates. Not session artifacts, not governance — operational references.

## What belongs here

- **Dimension definitions** — the research dimensions registry that scopes what the IL scans for
- **Classification specs** — decision specs for pipeline components (e.g., Form Router rubric)
- **Pipeline operating parameters** — calibration findings, form distribution baselines, threshold definitions

## What does NOT belong here

- **Session output** — delta reports, calibration data tables, loop reports → `operations/research-reports/`
- **Governance** — DDs, IB items → `project-management/`
- **Research data** — findings, sources, authorities → their respective top-level directories
