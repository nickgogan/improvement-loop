## What Changed

- **Decision recorded (not yet executed at time of this entry):** collapse the federation
  into one self-evolving engine. Session 116 was design/planning only — no restructure file
  moves were performed in that session.
- **Engine = `systems/improvement-loop/`** (folder name kept to avoid a ~16k-file path
  migration). Three altitudes:
  - Bottom — research (findings, indexed by the dimension registry incl. Dim 7 Evaluation, Dim 9 Governance)
  - Middle — per-artifact (Librarian concept docs + `/assess-*` / `/design-*`)
  - Top — whole-system (schematic library + `/audit-system`, future `/design-harness`)
- **Charter (root `CHARTER.md`):** vision/mission/purpose/values + explicit trajectory
  signals — not a rulebook. Operating rules stay in `CLAUDE.md` / `.claude` / IL `governance/`;
  design-wisdom (fractal-pattern, DBDO pipeline, vocabulary) becomes plain knowledge.
- **Household OS:** archive + lift its 7 Notion schemas and architecture docs into
  `knowledge/reference/` as design substrate for the later Notion custom-agent build.
- **Claude Build:** retired; opinionated-stack intent preserved as knowledge artifacts.
